Dorian Kalir

Koszul Algebras and Koszul Duality

Sep 16
Abstract: Koszul duality was originally defined for graded algebras over a field and has seen many different generalizations. This is in part due to the many different ways Koszulness can be understood. In this talk we will examine a perspective guided by the twisted tensor construction which points to a generalization of Koszulness by Briggs, Cameron, Letz, and Pollitz from the field setting to morphisms of local rings.

Zach Nason

Quasi-Gorenstein Morphisms II: Electric Boogaloo

Sep 2
Abstract: On Sunday, I talked about joint work with Andrew Soto Levins and Ryan Watson that characterized quasi-Gorenstein morphisms of DG-rings using the virtually G-small property. In CARS today, I'm going to go over the other half of our work, which deals with exact sequences in noetherian local rings, and how to detect exact sequences by measuring whether certain maps of Koszul complexes are quasi-Gorenstein.

Kesavan Mohana Sundaram

On the annihilators of local cohomology modules

Aug 26
Abstract: In 2011, Lynch conjectured that the Krull dimension of the top local cohomology module supported at an ideal I in a Noetherian local ring R, is same as the dimension of R/T, where T is the I-torsion submodule of R. In 2019, Hochster raised a question that, is the top local cohomology module of a local Noetherian domain with support in a given ideal faithful? In the recent years, some cool progress and counterexamples have been made on these questions. So, why not check them out?
